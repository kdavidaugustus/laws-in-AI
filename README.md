# 🇮🇳 India Legal Guide

A free, open-source project designed to help people quickly find
relevant Indian laws, sections, procedures, and official sources
during critical situations.

## 🎯 Goal

Make Indian legal information easier to find and understand.

## 📚 Coverage

- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
- Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)
- Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam
- Constitution of India
- Important Central and State laws
- Common emergency situations
- Cybercrime
- Road accidents
- Police procedures
- Official legal resources

## 🔍 Features

- Search by situation
- Search by section
- Search by keyword
- Old IPC → BNS references
- Simple-language explanations
- Official source links
- Emergency guidance

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This project provides general legal information and is not a
substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional.

In an immediate emergency, contact the appropriate emergency
service or local authorities.
